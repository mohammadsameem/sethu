# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)

## Overview
PMAY aims to provide "Housing for All" by ensuring a pucca house with basic amenities for eligible households. It has two components: PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin.

## Eligibility Criteria
- The beneficiary family should not own a pucca house either in their name or in the name of any member of their family in any part of India.
- The beneficiary family should not have availed of central assistance under any housing scheme from the Government of India.
- Income limits apply for different categories (EWS, LIG, MIG) under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) component in Urban areas.
- For PMAY-G, households are selected based on housing deprivation parameters in the SECC 2011 data.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance to construct a new house or enhance an existing one.
- Interest subsidy on home loans under the CLSS for urban beneficiaries.
- The houses provided have basic amenities like a toilet, electricity connection, and water supply.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card or Voter ID
- Proof of Income
- Bank Account Details
- Property documents (if applying for enhancement/construction on own land)
- Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM) number

## How to Apply
- **Urban:** Apply online through the PMAY(U) portal or visit Common Service Centres (CSCs). For CLSS, approach a bank or housing finance company.
- **Gramin:** Selection is based on SECC data. Gram Sabhas verify the eligible beneficiaries. Contact the local Gram Panchayat for inclusion if eligible.
