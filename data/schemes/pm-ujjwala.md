# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## Overview
PM Ujjwala Yojana aims to safeguard the health of women and children by providing clean cooking fuel (LPG) to poor households. It ensures women are free from the hazards of indoor air pollution caused by burning firewood, coal, or cow-dung cakes.

## Eligibility Criteria
- The applicant must be a woman aged 18 years or above.
- The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household.
- No other LPG connection should exist in the name of any family member.
- Must belong to any of the following categories: SECC 2011 list, SC/ST households, beneficiaries of PM Awas Yojana (Gramin), Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY), Forest Dwellers, Most Backward Classes (MBC), Tea and Ex-Tea Garden tribes, or people residing in Islands and River Islands.

## Benefits
- A deposit-free LPG connection.
- Financial support of Rs. 1600 for a 14.2 kg cylinder or Rs. 1150 for a 5 kg cylinder connection.
- First refill and stove provided free of cost to beneficiaries under Ujjwala 2.0.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card (Applicant and all adult family members)
- Ration Card or any document proving family composition
- Bank Account details (Passbook or cancelled cheque)
- Passport size photograph

## How to Apply
Applications can be submitted offline at any LPG distributor agency or online through the official PMUY portal (pmuy.gov.in). Applicants need to fill out the Ujjwala application form and submit the required KYC documents.
