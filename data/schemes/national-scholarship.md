# National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Pre-Matric & Post-Matric Schemes

## Overview
The National Scholarship Portal (NSP) is a one-stop portal for various scholarship schemes offered by different Ministries/Departments of the Government of India. Two major schemes are the Pre-Matric (Class 1-10) and Post-Matric (Class 11 to Ph.D.) scholarships for minorities, SC/ST, and OBC students.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a student studying in a recognized school/institution in India.
- **Pre-Matric:** Students from Class 1 to 10.
- **Post-Matric:** Students from Class 11 up to Ph.D. level.
- Minimum marks requirement: Usually 50% marks in the previous final examination.
- Family income limit: Varies by category (e.g., typically Rs. 1 lakh per annum for Pre-Matric and Rs. 2-2.5 lakhs per annum for Post-Matric schemes, subject to specific ministry guidelines).
- Must belong to the respective reserved category (Minority, SC, ST, OBC) or Economically Weaker Section (EWS) for specific schemes.

## Benefits
- Admission and tuition fee waiver/reimbursement.
- Maintenance allowance per month for hostellers and day scholars.
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of the scholarship amount into the student's bank account.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Educational documents of the previous year
- Income Certificate
- Caste/Community Certificate
- Bank Account Details (must be seeded with Aadhaar)
- Domicile Certificate
- Fee Receipt of current course year

## How to Apply
Students must apply online exclusively through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in). Institutions must also be registered on the NSP to verify student applications. New users need to register, and existing users can renew their applications annually.
