# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government-backed savings scheme targeted at the parents of girl children. It encourages parents to build a fund for the future education and marriage expenses of their female child. It is part of the "Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao" campaign.

## Eligibility Criteria
- The account can be opened by the natural or legal guardian in the name of a girl child.
- The girl child must be an Indian resident.
- The account must be opened before the girl child reaches the age of 10 years.
- A maximum of two accounts are allowed for a family (one for each girl child). A third account is permitted only in the case of twin girls as second birth or if the first birth results in three girl children.

## Benefits
- High-interest rate (revised quarterly by the government).
- Tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act (up to Rs. 1.5 lakh per year).
- Interest earned and maturity amount are entirely tax-free.
- Maturity after 21 years from the date of account opening or at the time of marriage of the girl child (after attaining 18 years of age).

## Required Documents
- Birth Certificate of the girl child.
- Identity and Address Proof of the parent/guardian (Aadhaar, PAN card, Voter ID, Passport, etc.).
- Medical certificate in case of multiple children born at once.

## How to Apply
Parents or guardians can open an SSY account at any participating post office or authorized commercial bank branch by submitting the required application form and documents.
